Putting nature on the books since 2005.

I am Renato Vargas, an economist. I build accounts, models and data tools that show how an economy uses land, water, energy and air, and I train the teams that will keep them running.

Task team leaders and project managers at the World Bank, the UN Economic Commission for Latin America and the Caribbean, the Inter-American Development Bank, FAO and UNEP have used that work to back research, persuade clients and support policy.
